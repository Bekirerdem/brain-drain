# Security policy

Brain Drain is an open-source x402 + RAG protocol shipped solo for the
Colosseum Frontier 2026 hackathon. We take security reports seriously
and respond inside the windows below.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **l3ekirerdem@gmail.com** with a clear write-up. Include:

- A short title and a one-paragraph summary of impact.
- Steps to reproduce on the live deployment
  (`https://brain-drain-iota.vercel.app`) or a local clone.
- Affected endpoint(s), commit SHA, request/response samples, logs.
- Optional proof-of-concept (PoC) — please keep it minimal and avoid
  any action that would compromise other operators' wallets, vaults,
  or settlements.

Do **not** open a public GitHub issue for security-sensitive reports.
Use email and we'll coordinate disclosure.

## Response

- Acknowledgement: within **48 hours** of receipt.
- Triage + initial assessment: within **5 business days**.
- Patch + public disclosure: coordinated with the reporter; usually
  within **30 days** of triage for critical/high-severity issues.

We credit researchers in the release notes unless they prefer to remain
anonymous.

## Safe harbor

Good-faith research on Brain Drain that follows this policy is welcome.
We will not pursue legal action or initiate law-enforcement
investigation against researchers who:

- Make a good-faith effort to avoid privacy violations, destruction of
  data, and interruption or degradation of services.
- Only interact with their own accounts/vaults or with explicit
  permission of the account/vault holder.
- Give us a reasonable time to fix issues before publicly disclosing
  them.
- Do not exfiltrate any data beyond what's needed to demonstrate the
  vulnerability.
- Comply with all applicable laws.

## In scope

- The deployed Brain Drain app: `brain-drain-iota.vercel.app` and its
  `/api/*` endpoints.
- The MCP server endpoint: `/api/mcp` (Streamable HTTP transport).
- The Supabase Postgres + Storage stack used for the vault registry,
  vault index files, settlement ledger, rate-limit buckets, and auth
  challenges.
- The `Bekirerdem/brain-drain` source repository.

Specifically interested in:

- Authentication bypass or privilege escalation through the
  Sign-in-with-Solana flow (`/api/auth/challenge`, `/api/auth/verify`,
  `bd_session` cookie HMAC).
- Vault impersonation, ownership confusion, or RLS bypass in
  `vaults`, `vault_settlements`, or `vault_feedback`.
- Rate-limit bypass that lets a single client trigger expensive
  embedding work (`/api/vaults` POST, `/api/v/[slug]/feedback`).
- x402 settlement injection or replay against the
  `/api/v/[slug]/query` route (the ledger has a signature primary
  key; if you can persuade it to record a transfer that wasn't
  generated by the protocol, that's a finding).
- Server-only env loader (`src/lib/env.ts`) leaking into the client
  bundle (we caught one of these previously, see commit `3d7ba1a`).

## Out of scope

- Issues that require physical access to a victim's device or
  third-party Phantom wallet compromise.
- Self-XSS, social-engineering, or attacks against the maintainer's
  personal accounts.
- Denial-of-service against the public Vercel deployment that requires
  no application-level vulnerability (rate-limit buckets are
  app-level).
- Findings against transitive dependencies that are already documented
  with a mitigation in the README's Security audit section
  (`bigint-buffer` 1.1.5 has no upstream patch; the read-only ATA
  derivation scope mitigation is intentional and documented).
- Issues that require the operator wallet's private key — this is the
  expected trust boundary; Brain Drain never custodies wallet
  material.

## Cryptography boundaries

Brain Drain does not implement custom cryptography. The auth flow uses:

- `tweetnacl.sign.detached.verify` for ed25519 over a server-issued
  nonce.
- `node:crypto.createHmac("sha256", AUTH_SECRET)` with
  `timingSafeEqual` for session-cookie verification.

If you find a deviation from these primitives or an misuse pattern
(short nonces, missing replay protection, etc.), that is in scope.
